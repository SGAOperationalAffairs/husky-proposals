Vote button ideas

Sixteen ways to show an unlimited, one-tap vote, in Husky red with square corners. Each one sits next to your outline Learn more button, the way it would on a card. Tap any of them to see the pressed state and the little animation.

What unlimited votes change. The button now means "I support this", not "this is my top pick". So the icon and wording have to read as a vote and not a like, and the count carries the story.

Counts then measure breadth, not priority. If you also want to turn supporters into helpers, the "Support plus I'll participate" idea adds a second, separate tap for offering to help. It is one of the sixteen below.

Width matters. On a card this narrow, buttons with a text label push Learn more onto a second row, while icon-only buttons stay beside it. Label length is part of the decision.
